## 2. What we collect

**Account.** When you sign in with Google or with an email link, we receive your email
address and a user identifier from our sign-in provider. We do not receive or store your
Google password.

**What you tell the service:**
- the ratings you give films, and the aspects you say you liked;
- films you save to your watchlist and mark as watched;
- films you hide, and films you mark "Not right?", with the reason you choose;
- the messages you sent to the assistant during the closed test, and its answers (the
  assistant is not offered at launch, section 4).

**What we compute.** A taste profile: scores on 14 film dimensions, calculated from your
ratings. Recommendations and match percentages are derived from it.

**How you use the service.** Which recommendations were shown to you, which films you
opened or acted on, on which screen and when. We use this to measure whether
recommendations work. It is linked to your account, not to your name.

**Technical data.** Our hosting providers record IP addresses, browser or device type and
request times in their logs, for security and operation.

**Stored on your device.** Your sign-in session, your language, and whether you have read
the data notice. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

## 4. AI processing

**At launch the service calls no AI provider.** Nothing you do is sent to an AI model:
- the assistant is not offered;
- "Why you'll like this" on a film page is either a sentence an AI model wrote earlier and
  we stored (during the closed test, for that account and film), or a sentence our own
  code builds from your taste profile, with no AI.

We do not send your email or name to any AI provider.

## 6. How long we keep it

- **Account data:** while your account exists.
- **Deleted accounts:** when you delete your account (Profile → Delete account), your
  ratings, watchlist, feedback, assistant history, usage data and taste profile are
  deleted at once, and so is your sign-in record with your email. [True once branch
  `account-delete-auth` is merged and live; until then README gap 1 stands.]
- **Provider logs.** Our providers keep request logs, which can include your IP address,
  for these periods (their own documents, read 2026-11-27, for the plans we use):
  - Render (our server): our server's logs, **7 days** (Hobby workspace plan).
  - Supabase (sign-in and database): API and database logs **1 day**, sign-in audit logs
    **24 hours** (Free plan).
  - Cloudflare (the website): Cloudflare states no fixed period; it keeps such data "for a
    period of time that is consistent with the business purposes" it lists. [Lawyer: is
