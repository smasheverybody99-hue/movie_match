## 2. What we collect

**Account.** When you sign in with Google or with an email link, we receive your email
address and a user identifier from our sign-in provider. We do not receive or store your
Google password.

**What you tell the service:**
- the ratings you give films, and the aspects you say you liked;
- films you save to your watchlist and mark as watched;
- films you hide, and films you mark "Not right?", with the reason you choose;
- the messages you send to the assistant, and its answers.

**What we compute.** A taste profile: scores on 14 film dimensions, calculated from your
ratings. Recommendations and match percentages are derived from it.

**How you use the service.** Which recommendations were shown to you, which films you
opened or acted on, on which screen and when. We use this to measure whether
recommendations work. It is linked to your account, not to your name.

**Technical data.** Our hosting providers record IP addresses, browser or device type and
request times in their logs, for security and operation.

**Stored on your device.** Your sign-in session, your language, and whether you have read
the data notice. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

## 4. AI processing

Explanations ("why you'll like this") and the assistant are generated by Google's Gemini
API. To do this we send Google:
- your assistant messages;
- a short description of your taste profile;
- the details of the films involved.

We do not send your email or name.
